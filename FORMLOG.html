The Form Tagging

The form tag in HTML is used to create a form that allows users to enter and submit information.

It can contain different form elements such as text fields, passwords, checkboxes, radio buttons, and buttons. The information entered in the form can then be sent to a server for processing.

Objectives

  1. To allow users to enter their personal account information.
  2. To demonstrate the proper use of HTML form elements.
  3. To create a simple and user-friendly account login form.
